The bakery

Three ovens, one corner, since 1998

Shirya opened on Alder Lane with a single deck oven and a stubborn refusal to use commercial yeast. The shop has grown a second storey and a pastry bench since then; the levain is descended from the same starter.

Everything is mixed the afternoon before, retarded overnight in the cold room, and baked through the morning so the shelves are full by seven. What doesn't sell, we take to the shelter on Moti Street at closing.

  • One starter, twenty-six years

    Fed twice a day, named Doris, and never once bought off a shelf.

  • Stone-milled, locally grown

    Wheat and rye from a farm two hours out, milled weekly and used within the fortnight.

  • Nothing baked for tomorrow

    If it isn't sold by seven, it goes to the shelter. We would rather run out than stale.
