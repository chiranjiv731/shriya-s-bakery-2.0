Allergies and all that

Everything here is baked in one kitchen, so we can't promise anything is free from traces of wheat, dairy, eggs or nuts. Tell the counter about an allergy and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help. Whole celebration cakes are made to order — 72 hours' notice and a conversation about what you actually want.
